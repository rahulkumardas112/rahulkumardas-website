ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Rahul Kumar Das

RKD

Author · Academic · Educator · Researcher

Rahul Kumar Das is an author, academic and educator from Tinsukia, Assam. He serves as Assistant Professor in the Department of Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College and is associated with academic research at Dibrugarh University.

His writing explores life, relationships, loss, self-worth, emotional maturity, consciousness, memory, purpose and meaning. His work often looks at the quiet experiences that shape people and the questions that remain beneath everyday life.

Academic Background

He completed his schooling at Ganpat Rai Rasiwasia High School, Tinsukia, followed by higher secondary and B.Com studies at Tinsukia College. He holds an M.Com in Accounting & Finance and an M.Phil from Dibrugarh University. His doctoral research focuses on the problems and prospects of DPIIT-recognised startups in Assam under the Startup India Initiative.

Teaching and Academic Work

His academic experience includes teaching Commerce as Guest Faculty at Dibru College, academic engagement with the Directorate of Open and Distance Learning and the Centre for Distance and Online Education at Dibrugarh University, and his present role in Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College.

Research Interests

His research interests include Accountancy, Finance, Stock Market, Management, Entrepreneurship, startups and government initiatives connected with regional economic development. He is particularly interested in understanding how institutions, financial access and entrepreneurship can influence opportunities for individuals and communities.

Books and Writing

Rahul Kumar Das has written books dealing with human experience, emotional life, relationships, identity, comparison, failure, loneliness, expectations, self-worth, purpose and consciousness. His work also extends to academic books, notes and learning resources for students.

A Growing Knowledge Space

This website brings together his books, academic resources, research, articles and future projects in one place. It is intended to grow as a space where readers, students and researchers can discover his work and follow new publications and academic contributions.
