ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Rahul Kumar Das

RKD

Author · Academic · Educator · Researcher

Rahul Kumar Das is an author, academic, educator and researcher, currently based in Dibrugarh, Assam. He currently serves as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College, Assam, and is associated with academic research at Dibrugarh University.

He was born in Tinsukia, Assam. He completed his schooling at Ganpat Rai Rasiwasia High School, Tinsukia, a government school. He later graduated from Tinsukia College, Tinsukia, with a Bachelor of Commerce degree. He subsequently completed his Master of Commerce in Accounting and Finance and M.Phil. from Dibrugarh University.

His academic and teaching experience includes serving as Guest Faculty of Commerce at Dibru College, Dibrugarh, and undertaking teaching and counselling responsibilities associated with Dibrugarh University, including its distance and online education programmes.

Academic and Research Work

His academic interests include Accountancy, Finance, Management, Entrepreneurship, Startups, Stock Market and regional economic development. His doctoral research focuses on “Problems and Prospects of DPIIT-Recognised Startups in Assam under the Startup India Initiative.” The research examines the development of recognised startups in Assam, including areas such as funding, government support, training, mentorship, regulatory challenges and growth prospects.

Books and Writing

Alongside his academic work, Rahul Kumar Das writes on subjects related to human experiences and contemporary life. His books explore themes including identity, relationships, love, loss, loneliness, comparison, failure, expectations, self-worth, emotional maturity, memory, consciousness, attention, purpose, time and meaning.

His published works include Where Silence Learned to Feel, The Things We Learn Too Late, Some Things Leave Without Leaving, The Silent Ache of Being Human, Beyond What We See, The Things We Never Learn About Being Human, The Guide Within, Becoming the Person We Needed, The Years That Broke and Built Us, Before the World Changed Us, The Life We Miss, Why Nobody Feels Enough, The Attention Crisis, The Invisible Line, The Human Shadow, The Psychology of Almost, The Life We Are Selling and The Human Need to Be Remembered, among other works.

Current Work

His academic and literary interests represent two areas of inquiry. His academic work focuses on commerce, economic activity, entrepreneurship, institutions and regional development, while his creative writing focuses on human behaviour, relationships, emotions and everyday experiences.

His connection with Assam is reflected in both his academic research and professional work. Born in Tinsukia and educated in Tinsukia and Dibrugarh, his academic and professional journey has remained closely connected with higher education and research in Assam. He is currently based in Dibrugarh and continues his work in teaching, academic research, writing and publishing.

Through this website, Rahul Kumar Das brings together his books, academic resources, research work, articles, educational materials and professional information in one place. His current areas of work include teaching, academic research, writing, publishing, entrepreneurship research, educational resources and the study of human experiences.
