ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Rahul Kumar Das

RKD

Author · Academic · Educator · Researcher

Rahul Kumar Das is an author, academic and educator from Tinsukia, Assam. He serves as Assistant Professor in the Department of Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College and is associated with academic research at Dibrugarh University.

His writing explores life, relationships, loss, self-worth, emotional maturity, consciousness and meaning. His academic interests include accountancy, finance, entrepreneurship, startups and research.
