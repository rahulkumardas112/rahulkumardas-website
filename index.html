ABOUT RAHUL KUMAR DAS

Rahul Kumar Das

Rahul Kumar Das is an author, educator, researcher and Assistant Professor in the Department of Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College, Assam. He is currently based in Dibrugarh and is associated with academic research at Dibrugarh University.

Born in Tinsukia, Assam, his work brings together teaching, academic research, writing and publishing. His books explore human experiences, relationships, emotions, behaviour, purpose and the questions that shape our lives.
