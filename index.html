ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Rahul Kumar Das

Rahul Kumar Das is an author, academic, educator and researcher from Tinsukia, Assam. He serves as Assistant Professor in the Department of Accountancy at Lakhimpur Commerce College and is associated with academic research at Dibrugarh University. His academic interests include accountancy, finance, stock market, management, entrepreneurship and startups, with particular interest in initiatives that contribute to regional economic development.

His doctoral research focuses on the problems and prospects of DPIIT recognised startups in Assam under the Startup India Initiative. Alongside his academic work, he writes books on life, relationships, loss, self worth, human behaviour, consciousness, purpose and the deeper experiences that shape everyday life.
