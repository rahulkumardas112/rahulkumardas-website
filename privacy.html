Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Information you provide

Depending on how you use this website, you may provide your name, email address, account details, order information, payment transaction reference, review content or messages sent through contact forms.

How information is used

Information may be used to manage reader accounts, process and verify orders, provide access to purchased digital products, respond to enquiries, moderate reader reviews and send updates you have chosen to receive.

Payments

Some checkout methods require a transaction reference for manual verification. Do not submit card numbers, passwords, one time passwords or other payment credentials in a review or contact message. Any third party payment service is subject to its own privacy terms.

Reader reviews

Review submissions may include your display name, selected book, rating and review text. Reviews are held for moderation. Only approved reviews are displayed publicly. Your optional email address is not displayed with the review.

Storage and security

The website uses hosted services to store and manage certain records. Reasonable technical controls are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your password with anyone.

Messages and updates

If you create an account or opt into updates, your preferences are used to manage those communications. You may contact the website to request help with your information or update preferences where available.

External websites

Links to retailers, social networks and other external services are governed by those services' own policies. This website does not control their privacy practices.

Contact

For a privacy related question, use the contact details or contact form available on the homepage.

This notice describes the website's intended data practices and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against the final payment, analytics and email services actually enabled on the website.
