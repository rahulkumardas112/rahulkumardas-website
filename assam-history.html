ASSAM · HISTORY · PEOPLE · PLACE

Understand Assam through the stories that shaped it.

A growing digital learning project to organise Assam's history, geography, cultural life and social change in a way that is accessible to students, readers and curious learners. The goal is to build a decade wise resource with clear explanations and references, not simply a collection of disconnected facts.

A CONNECTED VIEW

Explore Assam from different angles.

01 · Historical periods

Timeline and turning points

Build a chronological guide to major periods, political changes, movements and events, with each entry linked to reliable sources and further reading.

02 · Geography

Land, rivers and regions

Learn about the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys, hills, districts, biodiversity and the ways geography shapes settlement, livelihoods and connectivity.

03 · People and culture

Languages and traditions

Explore Assam's diverse communities, languages, literature, festivals, food, arts and cultural traditions with attention to context and different perspectives.

04 · Modern Assam

Society and development

Understand social change, education, economic development, institutions, public policy and the issues that continue to shape the state.

05 · Learning resources

For students and teachers

Develop concise explainers, study notes, reading lists, maps and video lessons that can support classroom learning and independent study.

06 · Sources and archives

Evidence behind the story

Each published topic should include its sources, distinguish established facts from interpretation and identify areas where historical accounts differ.

A careful project, built one topic at a time.

This page introduces the project structure. Individual historical articles and videos will be added as they are researched and prepared. Dates, quotations, maps and historical interpretations should be checked against credible books, archives and institutional sources before publication.
